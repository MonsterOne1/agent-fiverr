# Code of Conduct

## Standard

This project is for people building agent-native service infrastructure. Keep
discussion direct, technical, and respectful.

Expected behavior:

- Be specific.
- Assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect safety, privacy, licensing, and customer-data boundaries.
- Make uncertainty explicit.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Publishing secrets or private customer data.
- Encouraging unsafe automation of regulated work.
- Misrepresenting agent output as licensed professional advice.
- Repeated off-topic or bad-faith disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove issues, discussions, comments, or pull
requests that violate this standard. Repeated violations may lead to a ban from
the project.
